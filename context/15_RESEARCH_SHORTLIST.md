# Research Shortlist Before Product Selection

No final product selected yet.

## Candidate territory A — Autonomous Factor Discovery for rToken 7x24 markets
Why it survives:
- exact named Agentic sub-theme
- lower public S2 density than sentiment/event/cross-asset agents
- mature open-source patterns exist to reverse-engineer
- Bitget's 7x24 rToken/native-stock split creates unusual factor opportunities
- clear continuous product loop

Research questions:
- which historical rToken/US-stock/crypto fields are reliably available?
- how much point-in-time history exists?
- what factor DSL can be safely generated?
- how to stop p-hacking/multiple-testing?
- what promotion threshold can produce paper trades without cheating?
- how should factor decay trigger retirement/evolution?

## Candidate territory B — Portfolio-level overnight hedge agent
Why it survives:
- cross-asset named sub-theme
- product value is obvious
- existing entries mostly focus on pair divergence rather than whole-portfolio overnight risk

Risk:
- Gloaming, Triad, Crossfire, VIGIL, ARGUS already cover parts of the space
- differentiation must be portfolio-level risk target + autonomous execution, not another spread bot

## Candidate territory C — Durable event execution
Why it survives:
- common competitors use cron/loops
- durable restart-safe, idempotent workflows are less common
- Yield-paca shows this matters in a real trading system

Risk:
- reliability alone may look like infrastructure rather than a trading product
- must attach to a very clear end-user trading problem

## Current research preference

A > B > C.

This is not yet a build decision. The next pass should verify Bitget data availability and current public S2 Factor Discovery entries before locking the product.
