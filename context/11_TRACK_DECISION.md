# Track Decision Before Idea Generation

Snapshot: 2026-09-27.

## Under normal competition timing

Research Agentic Trading first.

Why:
- It is the most direct match to Agent Hub's execution architecture.
- The differentiated product can live above Bitget's tools rather than rebuilding exchange connectivity.
- It rewards both system architecture and actual trading evidence.
- There are still narrower problems around persistent state, cross-asset execution and autonomous factor discovery even though generic risk agents are crowded.

Do not use a generic "LLM reads news and trades with stop-loss" shape.

Sub-themes worth deeper research before generating ideas:
1. Cross-Asset Execution Agent
2. Factor Discovery Agent
3. Open Theme

Event-driven, market-sentiment and earnings agents have many obvious/public implementations.

## If the submission form is somehow still open right now

AI Trading Desk is the most feasible track for a very late start because the required proof is an accessible demo plus one complete research task.

A brand-new Agentic entry has a validation disadvantage because the handbook asks for competition-period paper logs and recommends at least two weeks.

Alpha Factory also has a high evidence burden: at least 60 days of backtest data and at least 30 days out of sample, with pure quantitative scoring.

This timing-based conclusion is separate from the normal best-fit conclusion.
