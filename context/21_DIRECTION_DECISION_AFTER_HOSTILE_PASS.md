# Direction Decision After Hostile Pass

Snapshot: 2026-09-27.

## Decision

Proceed with the Factor Discovery sub-theme, but discard the generic "AI factor factory" framing.

The working product category is:

AUTONOMOUS SESSION-ALPHA RESEARCHER FOR BITGET RTOKENS

It studies where factor behavior changes because tokenized US equities trade through regular-hours, after-hours, overnight and weekend sessions while the underlying market structure changes.

## 30-second explanation under test

"An AI quant researcher for Bitget's 24/7 tokenized stocks. It invents trading factors, tests every idea on unseen data and after costs, rejects the weak ones, and paper-trades only the factors that survive. When a factor stops working, it retires it and searches for a replacement."

## Why this survived

- exact named Agentic Trading sub-theme
- Bitget-native 24/7 rToken market structure
- no clearly found public dedicated S2 entry owning this exact end-to-end Track 2 loop
- strong open-source prior art to reverse-engineer
- clear separation from Playbook: autonomous research/falsification vs user-authored strategy deployment
- can remain useful after the hackathon
- supports honest "no alpha found" outputs
- can accumulate proprietary forward evidence over time

## Why the earlier version was rejected

The generic version was too close to:
- Microsoft RD-Agent
- Qlib/factor factories
- ARGUS factor lab
- GetAgent Playbook
- generic Alpha Factory research tooling

The narrowed product is about discovering and lifecycle-managing session-conditioned alpha in Bitget's specific 24/7 tokenized-equity market.

## Non-negotiable architecture

Bitget real market data
-> durable snapshot
-> Qwen research proposal
-> safe factor DSL
-> deterministic experiment engine
-> anti-overfit gate
-> Qwen lifecycle decision
-> deterministic promotion floor
-> forward paper portfolio
-> real Bitget marks
-> evidence/recovery ledger
-> decay monitor
-> next research cycle

## Winning standard

Do not optimize for feature count.

A strong submission should make five things undeniable within the first minute:
1. This problem exists specifically because rTokens trade 24/7.
2. The AI genuinely conducts research instead of narrating a fixed strategy.
3. Most hypotheses are rejected.
4. A promoted factor controls a real forward paper position.
5. Every claim is reproducible from the ledger.

The product should remain running after submission and keep accumulating research and forward evidence.
