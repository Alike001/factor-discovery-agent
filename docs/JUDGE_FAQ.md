# Judge FAQ

## Why are there no profitable factors?

Nine committed hypotheses were tested under frozen rules. None passed all gates. The product preserves that negative result instead of optimizing until something looks good.

## Why is paper trading locked?

Paper probation requires a Candidate. Candidate count is zero, so eligibility, allocation, positions, orders, and fills are all zero.

## Is Qwen actually central?

Yes. Qwen proposed the economic hypotheses and lifecycle explanation under bounded schemas. Deterministic code controls compilation, evaluation, gates, search accounting, budgets, and capital eligibility.

## How do you prevent overfitting?

The protocol fixes IS/OOS policy, fees, slippage, stability tests, 2,000-placebo permutation tests, Deflated Sharpe Ratio, baselines, provenance, and thresholds before evaluation. Every committed proposal increases global search N.

## Why does search N matter?

Repeated search raises false-discovery risk. The DSR hurdle uses the cumulative count across protocols; changing versions does not reset it.

## Why did proposals fail structurally?

Model output can violate a schema or request an unsupported operation. Those failures consume the permitted attempt and remain visible. Trial 9 could not be repaired within the frozen token budget.

## Why are raw ASTs no longer accepted?

Raw model-authored ASTs created a capability mismatch. Qwen now emits a small FactorRecipe, and deterministic code compiles only READY families into safe ASTs.

## What uses real Bitget data?

Instrument audits, trusted history, normalized closed candles, session labels, and deterministic experiments use Bitget Reality market data.

## What is replay versus live execution?

Replay reads stored evidence and makes zero model or market-network calls. Live research and trading are disabled.

## What would happen if a Candidate existed?

It would still require review, bounded target weight, risk gates, local paper probation, forward evidence, and lifecycle monitoring.

## What are the current limitations?

Trusted post-launch history is short; volume remains disabled; fees use an unverified baseline; Demo is unverified; Trial 9 never reached evaluation; and the public app is a snapshot rather than a live managed database.
