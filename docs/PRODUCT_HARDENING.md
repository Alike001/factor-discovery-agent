# Product hardening

Snapshot: 2026-09-28

The public product is a read-only, evidence-backed research system. Its central claim is narrow:

> The AI is allowed to have bad ideas. It is not allowed to turn bad evidence into a trade.

## Frozen state

- Search program: `rtoken-session-alpha-v1`
- Active protocol: `fdp-v3`
- Global search N: 9
- Committed trials: 9
- Candidate / Certified: 0 / 0
- Paper positions / orders / fills: 0 / 0 / 0
- Execution mode: `RESEARCH_ONLY`
- Evidence chain: 190 events, verified

No Qwen request, hypothesis, trial, threshold change, historical rewrite, paper action, or live trade occurred during hardening.

## Product surfaces

- `/lab`: final research state, controlled Trial 8/9 outcomes, gates, search control, and compiler boundary.
- `/factors`: all nine committed records, status filters, permanent failures, and evidence links.
- `/factors/trial-8` and `/factors/trial-9`: structured recipe and structural refusal proof.
- `/paper`: intentionally locked because no factor is eligible.
- `/ledger`: sanitized export of the append-only PostgreSQL chain.
- `/system`: last-verified source, provenance, compiler, budget, database, and execution status.
- `/proof`: the judge story in six short steps.
- `/replay?trial=8`: a stored-artifact replay with zero network and model calls.

## Presentation integrity

The structured recipe is authoritative. A deterministic formatter generates the executable hypothesis. Qwen prose is shown separately and never affects compilation. Trial 8 is explicitly marked `RATIONALE / RECIPE MISMATCH` because its structured `positive_continuation` entry conflicts with mean-reversion/decay language. The stored text is preserved.

Trial 9 shows only facts supported by evidence: Syntax failed, the projected repair exceeded the hard budget, and compilation/evaluation/anchor metrics were not run.

## Public evidence

`web/public/evidence/latest/` is generated by `scripts/export_public_evidence.py`. It includes required summaries plus a sanitized ledger and Trial 8 replay. The manifest pins the source research commit and hashes every file. Public output omits prompts, raw responses, reasoning content, credentials, authorization headers, connection strings, and private paths.
